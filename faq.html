How May We Assist You?

We provide complimentary express delivery on all orders over $50. Standard orders typically arrive within 2–4 business days via carbon-neutral courier. Expedited next-day courier delivery is available during checkout for $15.

We offer 30-day hassle-free returns on all unworn, unaltered items in their original packaging with dust bags intact. You can generate a complimentary prepaid return shipping label directly from your account or by contacting our Concierge desk.

Store your leather goods in the complimentary cotton dust bag provided. Avoid prolonged direct exposure to intense sunlight and moisture. Treat with a certified organic beeswax balm once every 6 months to preserve suppleness and natural luster.

Wash on a gentle cycle in cool or lukewarm water with mild liquid detergent. Line dry away from harsh direct sun. Linen softens naturally with each wash; steam while slightly damp for a pristine, airy finish.
